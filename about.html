Misha Lunia is an artist and ceramicist based in Indore, India. Clay is at the centre of her work: non-functional ware. She has been working with ceramics since 2025. She has a working knowledge of the wheel, hand building and glaze chemistry.

She picked clay because it is a physical medium. Touching clay grounds her and reconnects her with herself and the earth. She works in stoneware and earthenware from Bhoomi Pottery.

She works from her home studio. When she is uninspired, she goes to Studio Folklore, where it all started.

She is also an academic and engineer, trying to find her place between science and tech.
